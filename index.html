Legal help should be within everyone's reach.

LegalBridge connects citizens to safe, accessible and human-centred legal assistance — even when technology, mobility or accessibility becomes a barrier.

The problem

Real barriers stand between citizens and legal protection.

Distance

Legal Aid Offices may be difficult to reach.

Digital Barriers

Forms, CAPTCHA, OTP and documents can exclude people.

Safety

Unsafe communication can put vulnerable applicants at risk.

Accessibility

Digital access must work for people with disabilities.

How LegalBridge works

Key principles

The rules the product is built around.

AI assists, humans decide

Every consequential decision is made by a person, not a model.

Safety before convenience

No contact happens unless it's confirmed safe first.

Accessibility for everyone

Voice, phone, and human pathways exist alongside digital ones.

Incomplete information ≠ low priority

Missing details never automatically reduce a case's urgency.

Privacy by design

Sensitive details are visible only to the roles that need them.

Accessibility

Legal access should not depend on eyesight, internet or digital literacy.

LegalBridge is built voice-first, so that someone like Ripon — who cannot read a form, a CAPTCHA, or an OTP screen — can still report on behalf of someone he cares about.

Prototype scope

Built for the Legal Tech Hackathon 2026 · Accelerating Digital Legal Aid Services in Bangladesh (ADLASB) · UNDP Bangladesh · Directorate of Bangladesh Legal Aid (DBLA). Prototype only — not a live government service.

LegalBridge is a prototype concept for a digital legal-aid service. AI-generated information is not a substitute for official legal advice or human legal judgment.
